About Me — Gift Mugweni

Your everyday lover of knowledge, reading (fiction thank you very much), coding, and armchair philosophy that’s probably wrong.

Who is Gift Mugweni? I have no songs, movies or documentaries written about me. I have no life-changing money that will give me immediate social proof. I have no, as they say, claim to fame.

While I won’t say I’m average, I won’t say I’m one of a kind and unique either, I mean, I’ve met three people with basically the same face as me. What I do have though is my mind and my heart. I love reading stories that take you to fantastical worlds. I love learning more about the world and I’ve noticed reality is often stranger than fiction. I love relaxing on a quiet warm day and coding away at a dumb idea I have. I might never finish it but I’ll always love it.

Although I lay claim to my mind and heart, I still cannot say that is all I am. I was born and raised in Masvingo Zimbabwe, a little country located in the Southern African region. Some of you might think I’ll tell a story of how we grew up in a poor neighbourhood. I didn’t. My parents did though, and they worked very hard during their youth and made many sacrifices I might never fully know to ensure that by the time I was born, all I ever knew was the life of a typical upper-middle-class family. As a child, this lifestyle felt normal, but as I grew up, I started to understand what a heavy load they carried and how difficult their experiences must have been. This too is an aspect of myself for I learnt and incorporated many invaluable lessons from my parents and I continue to learn from their wonderful examples.

Is this all I am then? Of course not, for I’m also a Christian. Depending on who you are, this identity might spark a range of emotions from indifference to approval to annoyance, to villainisation, or other emotions I can’t think of. So, before we go further, I’ll say this to clear the room. I make no grand announcements that you are bad if you aren’t a Christian or that you are good if you are one. I’ve read the history of Christianity and whilst there are many examples of it doing good things, there have also been many atrocities committed by people of my faith both in the past and in the present. I grew up in a Christian family with Christian values. I find comfort in my faith, which has helped me overcome many tough times, but, I am also very aware that had I been raised in a different household, I might not have my current beliefs. It is not my right or responsibility to tell people what they should or should not believe in. I’m as flawed as any other human and quite frankly, the world is already difficult enough and doesn’t need us to manufacture more hate between each other.

With that rant out of my system, I’ll say that everyone is welcome to my little corner of the internet. I won’t push my beliefs on you so all I ask is you respond in kind. Whether you follow other faiths, are an atheist, or have a more complex and nuanced take on faith, that's ok. My content won’t focus on my faith much, but, I take pride in it despite its flaws and think it is important to mention it since it is a significant part of my life.

Moving on, my last significant identity is as a Software Developer. Programming has been a part of my life for at least ten years now and I intend to continue with it. I love writing code and solving problems. Although it sounds odd to some, I find you can learn quite a lot about a person's personality from how they write their code. Each program no matter how simple is laced with so much personality and history that I find utterly fascinating. The TODO that still needs to be done many years later. The comment stating the obvious. The group project held together by hopes and prayers. The hacked solution that was meant to be addressed but somehow got more love than the “correct” implementation. And of course my favourite response to a 100+ line pull request that’s layered with a lot of missing context: “Looks good to me”.

So … yeah. That's who I am. Just your regular guy trying his hand at writing. You can look forward to my writings mostly being musings of experiences I’ve had or observations I noted. I do have a technical blog you can find here but I want Medium to be the home for my more thoughtful and rambling content which I feel provides different value from my more “This is how you do X” content.
