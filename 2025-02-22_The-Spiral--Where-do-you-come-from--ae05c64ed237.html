The Spiral. Where do you come from?

A simple tale on the origins of this lovely spiral

The Spiral. Where do you come from?

It has come to my attention

That you might not know recursion

So I thought I should mention

You really should pay attention

Recursion is everywhere to see

Whether on land or in the sea

Some might charge you a fee

Thankfully, I’ll do it for free

Fibonacci is a favorite for me

A sequence that goes 1, 1, 2, 3

To get the next, add 2 to 3

Next then, add 5 to 3

A simple pattern, I hope you see

Add the last two numbers, the next is where you’ll be

But good sir, you might ask me

Where in nature is the sequence, you question me

Numbers are abstract, we often forget

They can mean anything, they need a target

Think of the sides of a square, you won’t regret

All sides are equal, a single number, there’s our target

Behold, we get a spiral

Everywhere in nature, you’ll see this spiral

Sea shells, flowers, insects, all use this spiral

Even your very ear, is this spiral

So, I heard you didn’t know recursion

I hope now, you’ll pay more attention

Now you see why I had to mention

You really weren’t paying attention
