Ah, to Be Older and Not Wiser

These are the ramblings of a guy maybe having a midlife crisis

I write this on the 11th of March. It's not a particularly earth-shattering day. No kings were born. No worlds were found. The AI apocalypse is yet to arrive. The daily modernity is still with us.

It's a simple day. It's a normal day. It's my birthday. I’ve never been one to go crazy celebrating my birthday. That doesn't mean I don’t value the day. I’ve just never really seen the need to yell to the world about it.

To some people, I’m still a young, ignorant fool who hasn’t understood the world yet. To other people, I’m old and ancient. It's a weird thing, being 28. I’m old enough to acknowledge that a significant amount of time has passed. I’m young enough to acknowledge I’ve still got a lot of time ahead.

It's a weird thing, being 28. I don’t feel any wiser. I don’t feel any smarter. I don’t feel more mature. And yet, when I think back to my younger days, I can’t say I’m still the same person. I’ve changed, but I can’t figure out when it happened.

Somewhere in the far reaches of time lies a younger me still unaware of what's to come. Somewhere in the far reaches of time lies an older me laughing at this “blogging” thing I’ve been trying to get off the ground.

Maybe I’ll succeed and look at this time in fondness. Maybe it’ll never really take off. Who can say at the moment? All I can do is give my best to live my life. All I can do is try my best in this life. I’ll bet on myself for that small chance. For if I don’t even bet on myself, then who will?

I’m reminded of the Thinking poem by Walter D Wintle

If you think you’re beaten, you are. 
If you think you dare not, you don’t. 
If you’d like to win, 
But you think you can’t, 
It’s almost a cinch you won’t. 
If you think you’ll lose, you’re lost, 
For out in the world you’ll find 
Success begins with a fellow’s will, 
It’s all in the state of mind. 
If you think you’re outclassed, you are. 
You’ve got to think high to rise. 
You’ve got to be sure of yourself before 
You can ever win a prize. 
Life’s battles don’t always go 
To the stronger or faster man, 
But soon or late, the man who wins, 
Is the one who thinks he can.
–Walter D. Wint

I think I can do this blogging thing. I think I can do this adulting thing. I don’t know what tomorrow will bring, but I’m curious to find out. I hope you’ll be there with me too. Thanks for reading my ramblings 😁. It means a lot to me.
