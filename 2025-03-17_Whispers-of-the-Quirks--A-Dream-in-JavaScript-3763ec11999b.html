Whispers of the Quirks: A Dream in JavaScript

In the murmur of the console’s gleam

Where logic drifts, a coder’s dream

JavaScript, a language so sublime

Yet fraught with quirks, that mock my rhyme

Oh, null and undefined, thy kindred seem

Twin phantoms haunting every scheme

Equality's trap, the == confound

=== thy truth, where sanity's found

Let not this escape thy sight

For in its shadows, dwells the night

Context shifts, a fickle guise

Beware the function's veiled surprise

Array and object, so mutable be

Yet const declares, their mutiny

A hoisting spectre, silent, sly

Binds var to realms, where echoes lie

Oh NaN, thou number false

The plague of sums, that halt and waltz

Infinity mocks, the bounds of time

As loops eternal, hum their chime

Yet, amidst thy chaos, beauty grows

A tapestry of code, that glows

For in thy quirks, thou dost inspire

A burning coder’s, endless fire
