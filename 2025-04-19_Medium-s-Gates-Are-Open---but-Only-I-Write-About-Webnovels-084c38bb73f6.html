Medium’s Gates Are Open — but Only I Write About Webnovels

Where cultivation arcs, dungeon crawls, and system prompts get the spotlight they deserve

Welcome to Chapter 999+. We’re a new publication whose sole purpose is very simple. To talk about Web Novels. During my admittedly brief time on medium, I noticed no one is talking about things I really care about. I might be alone, but it felt like much of the content I read on medium just talked about how I can get other people on Medium to read my stuff.

Is this not an important issue? Of course it's important. But I just couldn’t do it. I’ve tried now for months to be a productive member and talk about technical things, and share technical knowledge, but every time I did, my soul for writing died a little.

I just don’t have the heart and mind to constantly write about serious things like how to improve your career or the hot thing going on in my tech industry.

Most of the time, I don’t even read such content and instead get The Primeagen to do it for me via his videos. That got me thinking, what do I actually read and enjoy. The answer was novels. Fantasy novels. Web novels.

I’ve been reading webnovels extensively now for the past 5 years and I’ve reached a point where I can’t even watch a movie or TV series anymore just because they don’t offer the same level of engagement.

Once you’ve reached the stage where you can see entire fight scenes in your mind and all it takes is a single sentence, its hard to passively sit and watch another generic over-edited movie with painfully obvious CGI. Aside from that, the character development offered by text is just on another level. On TV, you really can’t get into spending long periods of time telling the audience exactly what the character is thinking. It’d be boring and painful to watch. But in books, you can fully assimilate into the character and see the world from their perspective.

You get to understand their thought process. You can see where certain thought patterns lead them to victory and also see how they can self-sabotage themselves and justify it in their minds to not take accountability.

You see the thought and care they take to defeat a difficult boss. Because the written text intently lacks spectacle, it needs to make up for it with other things. I love it.

I can’t get enough of this. So, I finally caved and decided to talk about the things I enjoy.

Sadly, I couldn’t find any medium publications talking about web novels. I understand, they’re not seen as respectable works. Many of the authors are treated as amateurs because of their intentional use of simple language to convey their stories. But, as a person who used to be a big book snob who cared for long archaic words that are not used in everyday life, I must say I find web novels refreshing.

There’s a lot of creativity going on in the web novel space. There are so many talented authors who’ve written such beautiful, touching, funny, absured, and gripping stories. I hate that they aren’t talked about more. They truly deserve waaay more fame than they have now.

Sadly, I’m just a single man without even an audience. I can’t do much to change people’s minds on webnovels. But, I figured I might as well try.

And that’s why Chapter 999+ came into existence. To talk about these beautiful stories in the hopes that others can give them love. To share my and hopefully other’s love of stories so insane, you’d have to read them to understand.

Welcome to the publication and I hope one of the articles you’ll find here will encourage you to give web novels a chance.
